Nexus page text for Preem Weaponsmith 2.5.0. Paste the sections below into the mod page (Description tab) and the file description. The mod flavour texts at the end are the in-game descriptions, kept for reference.

=== Overview ===
Have you ever wanted to add smart gun capabilities to your Masamune rifle? Wish your Sidewinder could fire just a little faster? Or maybe you simply wanted to reduce the recoil on your favorite gun. Well my fellow chooms, have I got news for you. This crafting mod adds a whole new layer to weapon crafting in Night City, letting you customize your crafted weapons and make them feel truly unique.

The mod adds a new panel to the crafting menu where you pick special mods that give the crafted weapon new capabilities.

The same panel is on the Upgrades tab: add, swap or remove Preem mods on weapons you already own, iconics included. Upgrading an iconic installs the chosen mods with the upgrade; on any other weapon (and on fully upgraded iconics) the APPLY button installs them at no cost. Evolution barrels (Smart, Tech, Power) stay crafting-only, since an owned weapon can't change its type. Removed mods are not refunded.

Version 2.5.0 is for game patch 2.5 only.

=== Barrel Mods ===
Smart Barrel, Tech Barrel, Power Barrel - turn the weapon into a Smart, Tech or Power weapon
Integral Silencer - weapon becomes silenced. It takes a barrel slot, so a muzzle brake can stay on the gun (it keeps the brake's stats), and every gun plays a suppressed shot: its own, or a similar gun's where the base game has none
Compensator Barrel - reduces recoil by 40%
Vented Barrel - eliminates horizontal recoil
Turbo Barrel - increases fire rate by 20%
Xtra Barrel - x2 projectiles per shot, each at full damage, x2 recoil
Smart-Link RAM - Smart guns track up to 3 targets and lock on 50% faster
Smart-Link GPU - Smart guns lock on up to 50% further, -75% targeting area
Turbocharger - Tech guns charge in half the time
Omega Charge - Tech guns take 2x longer to charge, but do +100% damage
Velocity Booster - Power guns do +25% headshot and +50% weakspot damage
Corkscrew Rifling - wider ricochet angles and better ricochet path correction

=== Trigger Mods ===
Full-Auto, Semi-Auto, Burst, Tech (charge), Tech Full-Auto, Insta-Burst, Ultra-Action. Each fire mode has its sound, including on weapons that never had that fire mode in the base game.

=== Mag Mods ===
Thermal, Shock, Chemical and Shredder Rounds (15% chance to cause Burn, Electrocute, Poison or Bleed), Extended Mag (+50% ammo), Quick Mag (30% faster reload)

=== Blade Mods ===
Thermal, Shock, Chem and Sharp Blade (20% chance to cause Burn, Electrocute, Poison or Bleed), Carbon-Fiber Blade (25% crit chance), Serrated Edge (50% crit damage), Deflector Frame (40% less stamina when blocking)

=== Handle Mods ===
Rubberized Grip (+1 hit to the combo), Lightweight Handle (20% less stamina cost), Tyger Grip (leap to targets), Booster Handle (sprint attack becomes a long dash)

=== Progression System ===
Each save keeps its own settings. Change them with the SETTINGS button on the crafting screen.
Mod unlocks: Sandbox (every mod available) or Weapon Blueprints (default: better mods need a rarer blueprint).
Mod limit: Sandbox (default: no limit) or Weapon Blueprints (a rarer weapon takes more mods; on the Upgrades tab the rarity after the upgrade counts).
Slot layout: mod slots on the weapon or below it.
With Mod Settings installed, its Preem Weaponsmith page sets the defaults a new save starts with.

=== Requirements ===
Cyberpunk 2077 patch 2.5
Required:
- RED4ext 1.31.0 or newer
- redscript 0.5.32 or newer
- Codeware 1.21.0 or newer
- TweakXL 1.12.0 or newer
Optional:
- Mod Settings 0.2.22 or newer: only sets the defaults for new saves; the mod works without it. Mod Settings itself needs ArchiveXL.
- EnhancedCraft (a build for patch 2.5): Preem mods also work on its weapon presets.

=== How To Install ===
1. Install the required mods above.
2. Download the zip and extract it into your game folder ("...\Cyberpunk 2077"). It adds r6\scripts\PreemWeaponsmith and r6\tweaks\PreemWeaponsmith.

=== Updating from 2.2.x ===
- Start a new save. Saves that used an earlier version of Preem Weaponsmith (or a game patch before 2.5) are not supported.
- Delete archive\pc\mod\PreemWeaponsmith.archive from your game folder. 2.3.0 and later no longer ship it, and if it stays it replaces the game's 2.5 weapon sounds.
- Replace both r6\scripts\PreemWeaponsmith and r6\tweaks\PreemWeaponsmith.
- CyberCMD and Cyber Engine Tweaks are no longer needed.

=== Updating from 2.3.0 ===
Replace both r6\scripts\PreemWeaponsmith and r6\tweaks\PreemWeaponsmith (2.5.0 adds a file to r6\tweaks). Saves from 2.3.0 keep working. A weapon that already has the Integral Silencer keeps it in the muzzle slot; apply mods to it once on the Upgrades tab to move the Silencer to a barrel slot and free the muzzle slot for a muzzle brake.

=== File description (2.5.0) ===
Preem Weaponsmith 2.5.0 - for game patch 2.5. Preem mods on the Upgrades tab (owned weapons and iconics). The Integral Silencer takes a barrel slot, keeps muzzle brakes on, and every gun plays a suppressed shot. Balance: weapons converted by an evolution barrel get their new type's damage, converted Tech weapons keep Charge, the Smart Barrel hits and flies like most Smart guns, and every Xtra Barrel projectile does full damage. Saves from 2.3.0 keep working; from 2.2.x start a new save and delete the old PreemWeaponsmith.archive.
